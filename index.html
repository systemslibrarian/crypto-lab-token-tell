Token Tell

SynthID-Text · C2PA · watermark vs. signature

Runs the real keyed g-value detector over watermarked text, then signs the same words with ECDSA P-256, so you can watch one kind of evidence vanish when the key changes and the other stay binary when a byte does.

WHY IT MATTERS

Schools, courts and newsrooms are being sold "AI detectors" that promise to tell them what a machine wrote. A watermark detector answers a much narrower question, and knowing which question was answered is the difference between evidence and an accusation.

Rebuilds every experiment from scratch and drops the settings the address bar is carrying. Nothing is reloaded: the depth, the section on screen and the scroll position stay where they are.

A watermark is not a signature. SynthID-Text does not detect AI text — it detects text generated by a model carrying your key.

More precisely: the detector runs a statistical test for evidence associated with a particular watermark configuration/key. It does not establish authorship and does not prove that text is AI-generated.

One watermarked passage, scored three times on load: under the configured key, under a wrong key given byte-identical input, and once on unwatermarked text. This replays the result.
